Evaluating AI Models

Choose metrics that match the task and its risks.

Artificial IntelligenceExplainerTechSphere

Overview

Model evaluation measures performance on data or situations not used to fit a model. The right metric depends on the task, class balance, error costs, and intended use.

What to remember

For classification, precision, recall, F1-score, and a confusion matrix reveal different aspects of performance. Keep test data separate from training and tuning, and document limitations.
