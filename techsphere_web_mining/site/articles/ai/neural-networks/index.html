Neural Networks Explained

How layers of simple computations learn useful representations.

Artificial IntelligenceExplainerTechSphere

Overview

A neural network contains connected units arranged in layers. Each unit combines inputs using weights and a bias, applies an activation function, and passes its output onward. Training adjusts parameters to reduce a loss function.

What to remember

Neural networks are used for images, speech, language, and forecasting. They can model complex relationships, but may need substantial data and compute. Validation helps reveal overfitting.
