Serverless Computing

Run code without directly managing servers.

Cloud ComputingExplainerTechSphere

Overview

Serverless platforms let teams deploy functions or managed services while the provider handles much of the infrastructure. Billing and scaling may follow request volume or execution duration.

What to remember

Consider cold starts, execution limits, observability, vendor-specific behavior, and data flow. Serverless reduces some operational work but does not mean servers do not exist.
