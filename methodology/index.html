Project Methodology

Our experiment compares how a crawler discovers public pages through ordinary hyperlinks and through a sitemap. We record discovered URLs, successful fetches, crawl depth, link relationships, and robots.txt decisions.

The crawler begins from a seed page, follows same-site links, normalises URLs, and avoids fetching URLs disallowed by the configured rules. Results should be measured from actual runs.

This is a static educational site. Project measurements and crawl outcomes should be reported only after running the crawler and reviewing its exported data.
