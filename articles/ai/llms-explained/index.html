Large Language Models

What language models learn and how they generate text.

Artificial IntelligenceExplainerTechSphere

Overview

Large language models learn statistical patterns in text and generate token sequences from context. They can summarize, classify, answer questions, and assist with writing or coding, but their output is not guaranteed to be correct.

What to remember

Check important claims, provide relevant context, protect sensitive information, and evaluate outputs against clear criteria. Fluent language is not the same as verified knowledge.

How this connects to our project

TechSphere provides a controlled collection of public pages for learning and experimentation. A separate Python crawler can discover this page through links, record its URL and title, and analyse its relationship to other pages. Any findings should be based on the actual crawl report.
